What this is. A feed on unidentified aerial and anomalous phenomena, unidentified submerged objects, and the question of non-human intelligence — worldwide, in 25 languages. Nothing here is written or summarised: headlines and snippets are the publishers' own, every row links to the original, and no model touches the text.

Standing. Every wire is labelled by what kind of source it is, and every row carries that label. Official is government and military: AARO, defence departments, released records. Science is research and journals. Press is general news. Specialist is independent reporting devoted to this beat. Sceptical is the work of explaining cases, which belongs here as much as the rest — a case resolved is a case answered. Standing says who is speaking. It is not a claim that a story is true.

Evidence. Separately, each story is scored on what it brought with it: a released document or FOIA record (2), sensor data — radar, infrared, sonar, telemetry (2), a hearing, journal or other on-the-record setting (1), a named witness or sworn testimony (1), a resolved explanation (1), and a primary source (1). At three or more the row is marked as documented, and the Evidence filter narrows to those. The pips show the score; the words beside them say what earned it.

What is refused. This subject attracts more nonsense than almost any other, so claims that cannot be examined are dropped at the door: channelled messages, galactic federations, reptilians, starseeds, ancient astronauts, hypnotic regression, predicted disclosure dates, and the usual conspiracy furniture. The word alien is also refused in its other senses — immigration law, invasive species, and the film franchise. Serious independent journalism is welcome; the esoteric is not. The status line reports how many stories were refused on each harvest.

Limits worth knowing. The gate is mechanical: it reads words, not meaning, so a careful piece written in loose language can be refused and a thin one written in careful language can get through. Standing is assigned per wire, not per article, so a weak piece from a strong outlet still carries that outlet's label. Google News caps a query at roughly 100 results over about 30 days. Coverage is uneven by language and the counts show it rather than hiding it.

Every wire read on the last harvest, with its standing: what it returned, and how many of its stories were refused as esoteric. A wire that could not be reached says so rather than disappearing.
